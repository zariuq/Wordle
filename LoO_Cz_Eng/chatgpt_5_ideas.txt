S = session number; Q = question number. Output is in numeric order with **CZ then EN** for each `S.Q`.

## Why the simple parsers failed

1. On the EN side, many sessions **embed `1.1`, `1.2`, … inside the big `1.0 (EN)` block** (the prologue). A header-only parser misses them. Session 1 clearly shows “…requesting. **1.1 Questioner** … 1.2 Questioner …” inline under `1.0 (EN)`. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}
2. Fake “empty” headers exist (e.g., `1.1 (EN)` with no body). If you prefer longest-text-wins, these empty stubs must **never** overwrite real text found inline.
3. Tokenization gotcha: `1.1` inside `1.10`. Use a regex that **does not** match `1.1` when followed by another digit.
4. Noise: both sites append large **footers/sidebars** (“Watch video”, “Glossary”, “Rychlé odkazy”, etc.) that must be dropped. They appear right after the real content in many dumps. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}
5. Odd numbering artifacts (e.g., `1 . 7` with spaces) also occur and should still parse as `1.7`. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

---

## Acceptance criteria (definition of done)

- For **Session 001**, the output **MUST** include non-empty rows for `1.1 EN` and `1.2 EN`. (They live inside `1.0 (EN)` in the source.) :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}
- For every `S.Q` that exists in either language, emit **two lines** (CZ then EN) with the available text; if one language is truly missing, keep the field empty (but do not invent or duplicate).
- Output contains **only** question lines—no session prologues, no site footers.

**Algorithm:**
1. Scan explicit headers to get language **blocks** (`S.Q (CS|EN)`). For each block, also include its trailing text up to the next header.
2. **Within each block**, split on the implicit `S.Q` tokens (the crucial step to recover EN 1.1/1.2/… embedded in `1.0 (EN)`).
3. **Language detection override:** start with declared block language; if the slice looks Czech (diacritics or “Tazatel”/“Já jsem”), force `CS`; if it looks English (“Questioner”, “I am Ra”), force `EN`.
4. **Merge policy:** for each `(S.Q, lang)`, keep the **longest** slice seen (prevents empty/short stubs overwriting a good inline chunk).
5. **Sanitize:** trim whitespace; drop lines containing any of these tokens:
rychlé odkazy, sociální sítě, o nás, šablona webu, nastavení cookies, ©,
watch video, listen, original audio recordings, glossary, unusual words,
entities, places, protection rituals, resource series, amazon kindle,
audiobook, youtube, facebook, instagram, read time

## Known edge cases (from our samples)

- Session 1 EN contains long inline text under `1.0 (EN)` followed by repeated “Watch video / Glossary …” footers; ignore those. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}
- Spaced tokens like `1 . 7` should still be recognized as `1.7`. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

## How we got here (summary of attempts)

- **Header-only split** → missed EN 1.1/1.2 because they’re embedded under `1.0 (EN)`; flagged as “missing” even though the text is present. :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}
- **Naïve token regex `\d+\.\d+`** → mis-parsed `1.10` as `1.1`.  
- **Fixes** → in-block splitting, negative look-ahead/behind in regex, longest-text-wins merge, and a small language heuristic.
